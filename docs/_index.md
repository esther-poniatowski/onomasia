# Onomasia documentation

Orients a consumer of Onomasia, or a tool exchanging documents with it, toward the specification or the guide serving its task.

- [Record formats](formats.md): lets a consumer write the glossaries, notations and overlays read by Onomasia
- [Configuration](configuration.md): lets a consumer adapt Onomasia to its records, its vocabularies and its outputs
- [Contracts](contracts.md): lets another tool rely on the documents exchanged with Onomasia
- [Using Onomasia](guide.md): shows a consumer how to run Onomasia on its records
- [Architecture decisions](adr/_index.md): records the decisions binding later work on Onomasia

> [!IMPORTANT] Formats and contracts
> The record formats bind the authors of records, whereas the contracts bind the tools exchanging documents with Onomasia.
